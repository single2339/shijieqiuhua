# 涛动周期论 — Kondratiev Cycle Thinking Skill

**Based on**: 《涛动周期论：经济周期决定人生财富命运》周金涛（中信建投首席经济学家），机械工业出版社，2017

---

## Core Belief

> **人生发财靠康波** — 财富积累不取决于个人能力，而取决于你出生在经济周期的哪个阶段。一生大约有3次重大机会，抓住一次即可成为中产阶级，错过全部则注定平庸。

---

## Three-Cycle Nesting Framework (三周期嵌套)

Schumpeter's model: three cycles operate simultaneously. When they align in the same direction, the signal is overwhelming.

| Cycle | Length | Driver | What it determines |
|-------|--------|--------|-------------------|
| **康波 (K-Wave)** | 50-60 yr | Technological revolution | Generational wealth, super trends |
| **朱格拉 (Juglar)** | 8-10 yr | Fixed asset investment | Industry booms, capex cycles |
| **基钦 (Kitchin)** | 3-4 yr | Inventory adjustment | Short-term timing, trading |

**Key insight**: Most analysts confuse which cycle they're in. A K-wave recession can have Juglar recoveries and Kitchin rallies within it. The skill is knowing which cycle dominates at each moment.

---

## Kondratiev Wave Phases (康波四阶段)

A complete K-wave runs ~60 years (Chinese 60-year Jiazi cycle):

| Phase | Years (this wave) | Character | Asset Performance |
|-------|-------------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Recovery** | 1982-1991 | New technology emerges, old economy restructures | Bonds > Stocks > Cash |
| **Prosperity** | 1991-2004 | Tech revolution fully deployed, euphoria | Stocks > Commodities > Cash |
| **Recession** | 2004-2015 | Growth slows but asset prices still inflate from liquidity | Commodities (1st half) > Real estate > Stocks |
| **Depression** | 2015-2025+ | Debt deflation, liquidity crises, asset price collapse | Gold > Cash > Bonds > Stocks |

**Critical framing**: Zhou's 2016 prediction placed us entering the depression phase. In depression:
- Central bank easing loses effectiveness ("放水不管用了")
- Asset returns turn negative for 4-5 years
- Liquidity crisis risk peaks around 2017-2019
- The correct strategy shifts from "maximizing returns" to "preserving past returns"

## Asset-Specific Cycle Laws

### Commodities (大宗商品)
- **Four-cycle nesting**: K-wave (50-60yr) → Capacity cycle (25-30yr) → Super cycle (18-20yr) → Oscillation cycle (3-4yr)
- Commodity super bull runs occur in K-wave recession (10-year bull, e.g. 2001-2011)
- After super bull ends: ~15-20 year bear market
- **Zhou's 2016 call**: 2016 = super rebound year (correct), 2018-2019 = final low (the bottom for strategic entry), 2030 = next super cycle starts
- Price pattern in depression: double bottom, the second bottom around 2019

### Real Estate (房地产)
- 25-30 year cycle, NOT globally synchronized
- Real estate is the **core carrier of growth** within a K-wave, but it **lags** technological adoption
- One K-wave contains ~2 real estate cycles: one strong, one weak
- Transmission pattern: Lead country → Periphery → Catch-up countries → Resource countries
- **Zhou's 2016 call**: "2014年之后不买房" — the 2015-2016 real estate rally was the last rebound of a bear market, not the start of a new bull. The correct strategy was to **sell, not buy**

### Gold (黄金)
- Gold is the **mirror image of economic growth** — it performs best when everything else is worst
- Performance by K-wave phase: Best in depression, strong in recession, worst in recovery and prosperity
- Three-layer attribute model:
  - **Long-term**: Credit hedge (inverse of US entity growth + USD hegemony stability)
  - **Medium-term**: Commodity attribute (follows commodity capacity cycle)
  - **Short-term**: Risk preference (inverse of global risk appetite)
- **Zhou's 2016 call**: Gold's strategic allocation window is opening. In the transition from recession to depression, gold enters a long-term bull market lasting 5-10 years in depression.

### Equities (股票)
- In K-wave recession, stock markets can still rally on liquidity (2009-2015 bull)
- In K-wave depression, liquidity-driven rally ends; stocks decline with earnings
- **Past 6 years (2009-2015) investment logic**: "宽松" (easing) = the only thing that matters
- **Next 4 years (2016-2019)**: Preserve, don't chase yield
- Stock selection within depression: focus on structural opportunities from industrial transformation, not broad market beta

## Reformed Merrill Lynch Investment Clock

Zhou's key critique: traditional ML clock ignores **currency/forex dimension** and **multi-cycle nesting**.

| Phase | Growth | Inflation | Best Asset | Critical Variable |
|-------|--------|-----------|------------|-------------------|
| Recovery | ↑ | ↓ | **Stocks** | USD trend, which country leads |
| Overheat | ↑ | ↑ | **Commodities** | Production capacity utilization |
| Stagflation | ↓ | ↑ | **Cash** | Central bank credibility |
| Recession | ↓ | ↓ | **Bonds** | Duration, credit spreads |

**Zhou's additions**:
- In a globalized world, US cycle and China cycle are out of sync — the asset to hold depends on which country is in which phase
- USD is the **pricing currency** for commodities and gold — USD trend often overrides domestic cycle signals
- The same asset class (e.g. emerging market stocks) behaves completely differently depending on USD direction

## Application Rules for Investment Planning

### When to be aggressive
- Juglar cycle bottom + K-wave recovery/prosperity → **Full risk-on**
- Real estate cycle start in lead countries → **Commodities super cycle beginning**
- Inventory cycle bottom within a K-wave recovery → **Stocks**

### When to be defensive
- K-wave recession → **Commodities for first half, cash for second half**
- K-wave depression → **Gold + Cash, sell real estate, avoid leverage**
- Any time "放水不管用了" (easing stops working) → **Get defensive immediately**

### When to buy gold
- K-wave recession beginning → start accumulating
- K-wave depression → overweight
- Before systemic liquidity crisis → buy before, sell into the panic

### When to buy commodities
- At the END of K-wave depression (around the final low point)
- The 2019 bottom was the generational entry point (per Zhou's framework)
- After entry, hold for the NEXT K-wave recovery cycle (10+ year horizon)
